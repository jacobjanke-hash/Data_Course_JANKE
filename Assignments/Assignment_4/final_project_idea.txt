Assignment 4 - Final Project Idea
===================================

1. PROJECT IDEA DESCRIPTION
---------------------------
My final project will analyze the relationship between study habits and 
academic performance in college students. Specifically, I want to investigate 
how factors such as hours spent studying per week, sleep quality, study 
environment preferences, and use of study techniques correlate with GPA.

The goal is to build a predictive model that can help students identify 
which study behaviors have the strongest impact on academic success, and 
to create visualizations that clearly communicate these relationships.


2. DATA DESCRIPTION
-------------------
The data I will use will include the following variables:
- Student ID (anonymous identifier)
- Weekly study hours (numeric, 0-50)
- Sleep hours per night (numeric, 4-12)
- GPA (numeric, 0.0-4.0)
- Study environment (categorical: library, home, cafe, mixed)
- Use of active recall techniques (yes/no)
- Major field of study (STEM, Humanities, Business, Arts)
- Year in school (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior)


3. DATA SOURCES
---------------
Potential data sources include:
- Survey data collected from students (primary data collection)
- Existing educational research datasets from Kaggle or UCI ML Repository
- Published studies on student academic performance
- University registrar data (anonymized)


4. EXPECTED DATA FORMAT
-----------------------
The data will be in CSV format with one row per student and columns for 
each variable. Example structure:

student_id, study_hours, sleep_hours, gpa, study_environment, active_recall, major, year
1, 15, 7.5, 3.4, library, yes, STEM, Junior
2, 8, 6.0, 2.8, home, no, Business, Sophomore
...

I expect approximately 200-500 observations for a meaningful analysis.


5. EXPECTED RESULTS
-------------------
I hypothesize that:
- Study hours will have a positive but diminishing return relationship with GPA
- Sleep hours will show an optimal range (7-8 hours) for best performance
- Students using active recall techniques will have higher GPAs on average
- The combination of adequate sleep and consistent study hours will be 
  the strongest predictor of academic success
